# Midnightsnacks.io  
**Private Dietary Disclosure for Safer, More Comfortable Dining**  
*A privacy-preserving DApp built on the Midnight Network*

---

## 🥗 Overview

Midnightsnacks.io is a confidential dietary-sharing platform powered by Midnight’s programmable privacy.  
It allows customers to discreetly share their dietary requirements, allergens, intolerances, and preferences with restaurants—without having to announce them publicly or reveal personal identity.

Users create an encrypted dietary profile and selectively disclose only the information they choose. Restaurants receive verified dietary summaries at the point of service or before bookings, enabling safer and more inclusive dining experiences.

To protect both customers and restaurants, menus and recipes are uploaded privately. AI tools analyse ingredients for allergens, hidden risks, and dietary compatibility without exposing any raw data.

---

## 🔐 Key Features

- **Private Dietary Profile**  
  Encrypted user data covering allergens, intolerances, dietary choices, and health or fitness goals.

- **One-Tap Selective Disclosure**  
  Customers can share only the necessary information with restaurants via QR scan or pre-visit submission.

- **AI Ingredient & Allergen Checker**  
  Restaurants upload menus and recipes privately. AI analyses ingredients for allergen conflicts or restricted items without exposing the underlying recipes.

- **Consent & Privacy Enforcement (Midnight Smart Contracts)**  
  Midnight enforces who can access dietary summaries, for what purpose, and for how long, with cryptographic proofs of correct handling.

- **Goal-Based Food Insights**  
  Optional analysis of macros or protein content to support personal health and fitness objectives.

- **Future Expansion**  
  Loyalty programs, private analytics, and menu optimisation tools for restaurants.

---

## 🎯 Problem We Solve

Research shows many individuals feel embarrassed or anxious when communicating dietary needs (allergies, gluten intolerance, halal requirements, vegan preferences, low-sugar diets, fitness goals, etc.).  
This often results in:

- Under-reporting  
- Unsafe meals  
- Miscommunication  
- Social discomfort  
- Inconsistent allergen compliance  

Midnightsnacks.io removes these barriers through private, trustless dietary disclosure and privacy-preserving food safety checks.

## 🏗️ How It Works (Technical Summary)

- **Encrypted Off-Chain Storage** for user dietary profiles  
- **Midnight Network Smart Contracts** for consent, policy rules, and selective disclosure  
- **Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs)** to verify:
  - restaurants only see permitted dietary info  
  - AI evaluation follows privacy rules  
  - raw user data and recipes remain confidential  
- **Private AI Processing** to detect allergens, preference conflicts, and nutritional characteristics  
- **QR-based or Pre-Arrival Disclosure Flow** for seamless dining experiences
